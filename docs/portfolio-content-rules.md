# Portfolio content rules

This file summarises public content rules for future portfolio updates.

## No invented claims

Do not invent profitability, Sharpe ratios, statistical significance, model accuracy, execution quality, live-trading activity, dataset sizes, latency statistics, completed functionality, repositories, publications, charts, backtest results or project links.

## Project-status definitions

- `Blueprint`: a structured design or research specification exists, but sufficient implementation evidence is not available.
- `In development`: some working implementation exists, but major modules, testing, results or documentation remain incomplete.
- `Implemented`: the stated functionality exists and can be inspected through code, reports or outputs.
- `Validated`: the implementation exists and includes meaningful testing, robustness analysis, documented assumptions and interpretable results.

## Homepage project-selection rules

Feature two to four strong records. Prioritise options, volatility and market-making work; systematic trading research and backtesting; ETF pricing or premium-dynamics research; risk systems or execution controls; and other technical work only where it adds genuine evidence.

## Writing-style rules

Use precise language that separates research questions, assumptions, methodology, evaluation, trading frictions, risk controls and limitations. Avoid generic marketing phrases and unsupported past-tense implementation verbs.

## Privacy restrictions

Do not publish Anthony's telephone number, original private CV, local or private document paths, personal document metadata, the 104.8% personal-investment-return figure, private financial account information or investment recommendations.

## Visual restrictions

Keep the design restrained, professional and recruiter-scannable. Avoid neon effects, fake trading terminals, stock tickers, decorative charts without data, generic finance photography, excessive animation and oversized technology-logo grids.
