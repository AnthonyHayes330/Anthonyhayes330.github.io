Quantitative approach

How I approach quantitative work

Assumptions before outputs

Models are useful only when their assumptions, data dependencies and failure modes are explicit.

Frictions before performance

A trading result is incomplete until costs, liquidity, implementation constraints and robustness are considered.

Risk gates before execution

A trading system should reject bad data and unsafe decisions before they reach an execution layer.
